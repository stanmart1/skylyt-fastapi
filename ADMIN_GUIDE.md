## Booking Management

### Understanding Booking Status
- **Pending**: Waiting for your approval
- **Confirmed**: Approved and paid for
- **In Progress**: Customer is currently using the service
- **Completed**: Service finished successfully
- **Cancelled**: Booking was cancelled

### Managing Bookings

#### To See All Bookings:
1. Click "Bookings" in the left menu
2. You can filter by:
   - Date range
   - Booking status
   - Customer name
   - Service type (hotel or car)

#### To View Booking Details:
1. Click on any booking from the list
2. You'll see:
   - Customer information
   - What they booked (hotel/car details)
   - Payment status
   - Special requests
   - All communication history

#### Creating a Booking for a Customer:
1. Click "New Booking" button
2. Choose service type (Hotel, Car, or Both)
3. Enter customer details
4. Select dates and preferences
5. The system will calculate the price
6. Choose payment method or mark as pending
7. Send confirmation email to customer

#### Handling Cancellations:
1. Find the booking you need to cancel
2. Click "Cancel Booking"
3. Choose the reason for cancellation
4. The system will calculate any refund amount
5. Process the refund if applicable
6. Customer will be notified automatically

## Car Fleet Management

### Understanding Car Status
Each car in your fleet has a status:
- **Available**: Ready for customers to book
- **Booked**: Someone has reserved it
- **Out with Customer**: Currently being used
- **Maintenance**: Being repaired or serviced
- **Out of Service**: Not available for booking

### Adding a New Car
1. Click "Fleet Management" in the left menu
2. Click the "Add New Car" button
3. Fill in the car details:
   - **Car Name**: What customers will see (e.g., "Toyota Camry 2023")
   - **Make & Model**: Toyota, Camry
   - **Year**: 2023
   - **License Plate**: The car's plate number
   - **Category**: Economy, Luxury, SUV, etc.
   - **Seats**: How many people it can carry
   - **Transmission**: Manual or Automatic
   - **Fuel Type**: Petrol, Diesel, Electric

4. Set the daily rental price
5. Upload photos of the car (at least 3 good photos)
6. Add any special features (GPS, child seat, etc.)
7. Click "Save"

### Managing Your Cars

#### To Edit a Car:
1. Find the car in your fleet list
2. Click "Edit"
3. Make your changes
4. Click "Save Changes"

#### To Change Car Availability:
1. Find the car in the list
2. Click the status dropdown
3. Select the new status
4. Add a note if needed (like "In for service")

### Car Maintenance Tracking

#### Adding Maintenance Records:
1. Click on a car from your fleet
2. Go to the "Maintenance" tab
3. Click "Add Maintenance Record"
4. Fill in:
   - What was done (oil change, tire replacement, etc.)
   - Date of service
   - Cost
   - Next service due date
5. Save the record

#### Important Documents:
Keep track of:
- Insurance expiry dates
- Registration renewal dates
- Roadworthiness certificates
- The system will remind you when these are due

## Payment Management

### Understanding Payment Status
- **Pending**: Waiting for your approval
- **Processing**: Being verified right now
- **Completed**: Payment successful
- **Failed**: Payment didn't work
- **Refunded**: Money returned to customer

### Payment Methods Available
1. **Credit/Debit Cards**: Processed automatically through Stripe
2. **Flutterwave**: African payment methods
3. **Paystack**: Nigerian payment solutions
4. **PayPal**: International payments
5. **Bank Transfer**: Customers upload proof, you verify manually

### Managing Payments

#### Verifying Bank Transfers:
1. Click "Payments" in the left menu
2. Look for payments with "Pending" status
3. Click on the payment to see details
4. Check the uploaded proof of payment
5. Verify the amount matches the booking
6. Click "Approve" or "Reject"
7. Customer will be notified automatically

#### Processing Refunds:
1. Find the payment you need to refund
2. Click "Process Refund"
3. Enter the refund amount (can be partial or full)
4. Add a reason for the refund
5. Click "Process"
6. Money will be returned through the original payment method

#### Payment Reports:
- See daily, weekly, and monthly revenue
- Check which payment methods are most popular
- Track failed payments and refunds
- Export payment data for your accountant

## Help & Support

### Common Problems and Solutions

#### "I Can't See a Customer's Payment"
1. Go to "Payments" in the left menu
2. Use the search box to find the customer's name
3. Check if the payment status is "Pending" - this means it needs your approval
4. If it's a bank transfer, look for uploaded proof of payment

#### "A Customer Says They Paid But I Don't See It"
1. Check the "Payments" section
2. Look for "Pending" payments
3. If it's a bank transfer, check if they uploaded proof
4. Contact the customer to confirm payment details

#### "I Need to Cancel a Booking"
1. Go to "Bookings"
2. Find the booking
3. Click "Cancel Booking"
4. Choose the reason for cancellation
5. The system will automatically process any refund

#### "A Car is Showing as Available But It's Actually Booked"
1. Go to "Fleet Management"
2. Find the car
3. Click "Edit"
4. Change the status to "Booked" or "Out with Customer"
5. Save the changes

## 💰 **Business Model**

### Revenue Streams

#### 1. Commission-Based Revenue (Primary)
- **Hotel Bookings**: 10-15% commission per booking
- **Car Rentals**: 15-20% commission per rental
- **Average Commission**: 12% across all services

#### 2. Service Fees
- **Processing Fees**: 2-3% on all transactions
- **Booking Fees**: Fixed fee per reservation
- **Cancellation Fees**: Percentage-based penalties

#### 3. Premium Features (Future)
- **Priority Support**: $10/month subscription
- **Exclusive Deals**: Premium member pricing
- **Corporate Accounts**: Volume-based pricing tiers

#### 4. Partnership Revenue
- **Insurance Products**: Commission on travel insurance
- **Airport Transfers**: Partnership with transport services
- **Experience Packages**: Tours and activities booking

- **Gross Margin**: 85%

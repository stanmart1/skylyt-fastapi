## Getting Started

### Welcome to Skylyt Luxury
Skylyt Luxury is your one-stop platform for booking luxury hotels and renting premium cars. Whether you're planning a business trip, vacation, or special occasion, we make it easy to find and book the perfect accommodations and transportation.

### What You Can Do
- **Book Hotels**: Find and reserve rooms at luxury hotels
- **Rent Cars**: Choose from our premium fleet of vehicles
- **Bundle Deals**: Save money by booking hotel and car together
- **Manage Bookings**: View, modify, or cancel your reservations
- **Track Payments**: See all your payment history and receipts

### How to Use This Website
- **Search**: Use the search boxes on the homepage to find what you need
- **Filter**: Narrow down results by price, location, features, and more
- **Book**: Reserve your hotel or car with just a few clicks
- **Pay**: Choose from multiple secure payment options
- **Enjoy**: Receive confirmations and enjoy your trip!

## Creating Your Account

### Why Create an Account?
- Save your favorite hotels and cars
- View all your booking history in one place
- Get faster checkout for future bookings
- Receive special offers and discounts
- Track your payments and receipts

### How to Sign Up
1. Click "Register" in the top right corner of the website
2. Fill in your information:
   - **First Name**: Your first name
   - **Last Name**: Your last name
   - **Email**: Your email address (this will be your username)
   - **Phone**: Your phone number
   - **Password**: Create a strong password
3. Click "Create Account"
4. Check your email for a confirmation message
5. Click the link in the email to verify your account

### Logging In
1. Click "Login" in the top right corner
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You'll be taken to your personal dashboard

### Forgot Your Password?
1. Click "Login" then "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Click the link in the email
5. Create a new password

## Searching for Services

### Using the Homepage Search
The homepage has two search options:

#### For Hotels:
1. Click the "Book a Hotel" tab
2. Choose your destination from the dropdown
3. Select your check-in date
4. Select your check-out date
5. Click "Search Hotels"

#### For Cars:
1. Click the "Rent a Car" tab
2. Choose your pickup location
3. Select your pickup date
4. Select your return date
5. Click "Search Cars"

### Advanced Search Options
Once you see search results, you can filter by:

#### Hotel Filters:
- **Price Range**: Set your budget
- **Star Rating**: 1 to 5 stars
- **Amenities**: Pool, WiFi, Restaurant, Spa, etc.
- **Room Type**: Single, Double, Suite, etc.
- **Location**: Specific areas within your destination

#### Car Filters:
- **Price Range**: Daily rental cost
- **Car Type**: Economy, Luxury, SUV, etc.
- **Transmission**: Manual or Automatic
- **Fuel Type**: Petrol, Diesel, Electric
- **Features**: GPS, Child Seat, etc.
- **Seats**: Number of passengers

### Search Tips
- **Be Flexible**: Try different dates to find better prices
- **Book Early**: Better selection and prices for advance bookings
- **Compare Options**: Look at multiple hotels or cars before deciding
- **Read Reviews**: Check what other customers say
- **Check Locations**: Make sure the location works for your plans

## Booking Hotels

### How to Book a Hotel
1. **Search** for hotels using the homepage search
2. **Browse Results**: Look through the available hotels
3. **View Details**: Click on any hotel to see more information
4. **Check Availability**: Confirm your dates are available
5. **Select Room**: Choose your room type
6. **Review Booking**: Check all details are correct
7. **Make Payment**: Choose your payment method
8. **Get Confirmation**: Receive booking confirmation via email

### Hotel Information You'll See
- **Photos**: Multiple images of the hotel and rooms
- **Description**: Details about the hotel and its facilities
- **Location**: Address and map showing where it is
- **Amenities**: What's included (WiFi, breakfast, pool, etc.)
- **Room Types**: Different room options and their features
- **Price**: Cost per night and total cost
- **Reviews**: Ratings and comments from other guests

### Understanding Hotel Pricing
- **Base Price**: The room rate per night
- **Taxes**: Government taxes (automatically added)
- **Service Charges**: Hotel service fees
- **Total Price**: Everything included - what you'll actually pay

### Special Requests
When booking, you can request:
- **Room Preferences**: High floor, quiet room, etc.
- **Bed Type**: King size, twin beds, etc.
- **Accessibility**: Wheelchair accessible rooms
- **Special Occasions**: Honeymoon, birthday, anniversary
- **Dietary Requirements**: For breakfast or room service

## Renting Cars

### How to Rent a Car
1. **Search** for cars using the homepage search
2. **Browse Available Cars**: See what's available for your dates
3. **View Car Details**: Click on any car to see more information
4. **Check Features**: Make sure it has what you need
5. **Select Dates**: Confirm pickup and return times
6. **Review Rental**: Check all details are correct
7. **Make Payment**: Choose your payment method
8. **Get Confirmation**: Receive rental confirmation via email

### Car Information You'll See
- **Photos**: Multiple images of the car (exterior and interior)
- **Specifications**: Make, model, year, and technical details
- **Features**: GPS, air conditioning, Bluetooth, etc.
- **Capacity**: Number of seats and luggage space
- **Fuel Policy**: Full-to-full, full-to-empty, etc.
- **Price**: Daily rate and total rental cost
- **Pickup Location**: Where to collect the car

### Understanding Car Rental Pricing
- **Daily Rate**: Cost per day
- **Insurance**: Optional coverage (recommended)
- **Fuel**: May be included or separate
- **Extras**: GPS, child seats, additional drivers
- **Total Cost**: Everything included

### Car Rental Requirements
- **Valid Driver's License**: Must be current and valid
- **Minimum Age**: Usually 21 years old (may vary)
- **Credit Card**: For security deposit
- **Insurance**: Proof of coverage or purchase our insurance

### Pickup and Return
- **Pickup**: Arrive at the specified location and time
- **Inspection**: Check the car condition with our staff
- **Documentation**: Sign rental agreement and take photos
- **Return**: Bring the car back on time and in good condition
- **Final Check**: Staff will inspect the car upon return

## Bundle Deals

### What Are Bundle Deals?
Bundle deals let you book a hotel and car together for extra savings. Instead of booking them separately, you can get both at a discounted price.

### How Much Can You Save?
- **Typical Savings**: 10-20% off the combined price
- **Special Promotions**: Sometimes even bigger discounts
- **Longer Stays**: More savings for longer trips
- **Premium Services**: Better deals on luxury options

### How to Book a Bundle
1. **Use Bundle Search**: Look for "Hotel + Car" option on homepage
2. **Choose Destination**: Where you want to go
3. **Select Dates**: Your travel dates
4. **Browse Combinations**: See hotel and car combinations
5. **Compare Savings**: See how much you save vs. booking separately
6. **Select Bundle**: Choose your preferred hotel and car
7. **Complete Booking**: Make payment for both services

### Bundle Benefits
- **One Booking**: Manage hotel and car in one reservation
- **Coordinated Timing**: Pickup times aligned with hotel check-in
- **Single Payment**: Pay for everything at once
- **Combined Support**: One point of contact for any issues
- **Better Rates**: Exclusive bundle pricing

## Making Payments

### Available Payment Methods
We accept multiple payment options for your convenience:

#### 1. Credit/Debit Cards
- **Visa, Mastercard, American Express**
- **Instant Processing**: Immediate confirmation
- **Secure**: Protected by advanced encryption
- **International**: Works worldwide

#### 2. Flutterwave
- **African Payment Methods**: Mobile money, bank transfers

- **Fast Processing**: Quick confirmation

#### 3. Paystack
- **Nigerian Payment Solutions**: Bank transfers, cards, USSD
- **Naira Payments**: Pay in Nigerian Naira

#### 4. PayPal
- **International**: Available worldwide
- **Secure**: PayPal buyer protection
- **Convenient**: Use your PayPal balance or linked accounts

#### 5. Bank Transfer
- **Direct Transfer**: Pay directly from your bank account
- **Proof Required**: Upload proof of payment

1. **Select Bank Transfer** during checkout
2. **Get Bank Details**: We'll show you our bank account information
3. **Make Transfer**: Use your bank's app or visit a branch
4. **Upload Proof**: Take a photo of your receipt or transfer confirmation
5. **Wait for Verification**: We'll confirm your payment within 24-48 hours
6. **Get Confirmation**: Receive booking confirmation once verified

### Payment Security
- **SSL Encryption**: All payments are encrypted
- **PCI Compliance**: We meet international security standards
- **No Storage**: We don't store your card details
- **Fraud Protection**: Advanced fraud detection systems

### Payment Confirmation
After successful payment:
- **Instant Email**: Confirmation sent to your email
- **Booking Reference**: Unique number for your reservation
- **Receipt**: Detailed payment receipt
- **Dashboard Update**: Booking appears in your account

## Managing Your Bookings

### Viewing Your Bookings
1. **Log In** to your account
2. **Go to Dashboard**: Click "Dashboard" in the top menu
3. **See All Bookings**: View current and past reservations
4. **Filter Options**: Sort by date, type, or status

### Booking Status Explained
- **Confirmed**: Your booking is confirmed and paid
- **Pending**: Waiting for payment confirmation
- **In Progress**: You're currently using the service
- **Completed**: Service finished successfully
- **Cancelled**: Booking was cancelled

### Booking Details
Click on any booking to see:
- **Service Details**: Hotel or car information
- **Dates and Times**: When your service starts and ends
- **Payment Information**: What you paid and how
- **Contact Details**: How to reach the hotel or car rental location
- **Special Requests**: Any special arrangements you made

### Modifying Your Booking

#### What You Can Change:
- **Dates**: Subject to availability and fees
- **Room Type**: Upgrade or downgrade (price difference applies)
- **Special Requests**: Add or modify requests
- **Contact Information**: Update your details

#### How to Make Changes:
1. **Find Your Booking**: Go to your dashboard
2. **Click "Modify"**: On the booking you want to change
3. **Select Changes**: Choose what you want to modify
4. **Check Availability**: Confirm new options are available
5. **Pay Difference**: If there's a price change
6. **Get New Confirmation**: Updated booking details

### Cancelling Your Booking

#### Cancellation Policy:
- **Free Cancellation**: Usually 24-48 hours before service
- **Partial Refund**: Cancellations within the free period
- **No Refund**: Very late cancellations or no-shows
- **Emergency**: Special consideration for emergencies

#### How to Cancel:
1. **Go to Your Booking**: Find it in your dashboard
2. **Click "Cancel"**: Select the cancellation option
3. **Choose Reason**: Tell us why you're cancelling
4. **Confirm Cancellation**: Confirm you want to cancel
5. **Refund Processing**: Any refund will be processed automatically

### Getting Help with Your Booking
- **Contact Information**: Each booking shows contact details
- **Customer Support**: Available through your dashboard
- **Emergency Contact**: 24/7 support for urgent issues
- **Live Chat**: Available during business hours

## Help & Support

### Common Questions

#### "I Haven't Received My Booking Confirmation"
1. **Check Your Email**: Look in your inbox and spam folder
2. **Check Your Dashboard**: Log in to see if the booking appears there
3. **Verify Email Address**: Make sure you entered the correct email
4. **Contact Support**: If you still don't see it, contact us

#### "I Need to Change My Booking Dates"
1. **Log In** to your account
2. **Find Your Booking** in your dashboard
3. **Click "Modify"** and select new dates
4. **Check Availability** and pay any price difference
5. **Get New Confirmation** with updated details

#### "My Payment Failed"
1. **Check Your Card Details**: Make sure all information is correct
2. **Check Your Bank Balance**: Ensure you have sufficient funds
3. **Try a Different Card**: Use another payment method
4. **Contact Your Bank**: They may have blocked the transaction
5. **Contact Support**: We can help troubleshoot payment issues

#### "I Can't Find My Booking"
1. **Check All Email Addresses**: You might have used a different email
2. **Search by Phone Number**: Try logging in with your phone number
3. **Check Booking Reference**: Look for the confirmation number
4. **Contact Support**: We can help locate your booking

#### "I Need to Cancel My Booking"
1. **Check Cancellation Policy**: See if you can cancel for free
2. **Go to Your Dashboard**: Find the booking you want to cancel
3. **Click "Cancel"**: Follow the cancellation process
4. **Confirm Cancellation**: Complete the cancellation request
5. **Refund Processing**: Any refund will be processed automatically
